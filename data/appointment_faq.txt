APPOINTMENT FAQ

Q: How can I book an appointment?
A: Select the required department and doctor, choose an available date and time, enter the patient details, and confirm the appointment.

Q: Can I choose a doctor?
A: Yes. The simulated system displays doctors according to the selected department.

Q: Can I choose an appointment time?
A: Yes. The system displays available appointment slots for the selected doctor and date.

Q: What happens after booking?
A: The selected slot is marked unavailable and an appointment confirmation is generated.

Q: Can two patients book the same slot?
A: No. Once a slot is booked, it becomes unavailable.

Q: What information is required?
A: The system requires the patient's name and phone number along with the selected doctor, date, and time.

Q: Is this a real hospital appointment system?
A: No. This is a simulated appointment system developed for educational purposes.

Q: What should I do in an emergency?
A: Emergency medical conditions should not be handled through routine appointment scheduling. Seek appropriate emergency medical assistance.
