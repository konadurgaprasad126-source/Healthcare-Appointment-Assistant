HOSPITAL POLICIES

1. Appointments should be booked in advance.
2. Patients should arrive 15 minutes before their appointment.
3. Patients should carry a valid identification document.
4. Appointment availability depends on the selected doctor and date.
5. Emergency medical conditions should be handled by emergency services and not through routine appointment scheduling.
6. Patients can contact the hospital reception for appointment-related assistance.
7. Appointment changes depend on available slots.
8. Hospital services and doctor availability may change.
9. Patients should provide accurate contact information when booking.
10. This system is a simulated appointment system for educational purposes.
