HOSPITAL SERVICES

The hospital provides the following departments and services.

1. General Medicine
General physicians provide consultation for common health problems such as fever, cold, cough, weakness, and routine health checkups.

2. Cardiology
The Cardiology department provides consultation related to heart and cardiovascular concerns.

3. Dermatology
The Dermatology department provides consultation for skin, hair, acne, rashes, itching, and related concerns.

4. Dentistry
The Dentistry department provides dental consultation for tooth pain, teeth problems, gum problems, and routine dental care.

5. Orthopedics
The Orthopedics department provides consultation for bone, joint, muscle, knee, back, and mobility-related problems.

6. Neurology
The Neurology department provides consultation for neurological concerns such as headaches, migraines, nerve-related problems, and memory concerns.

7. Appointment Service
Patients can use the simulated appointment system to select a department, doctor, date, and available time slot.

8. Doctor Availability
Doctor availability is based on the simulated appointment database. Available slots can change after an appointment is booked.

9. Emergency Service
Emergency medical conditions should be directed to appropriate emergency medical services rather than routine appointment scheduling.
